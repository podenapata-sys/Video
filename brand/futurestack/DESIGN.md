# FutureStack Solutions Design System

## Overview

AI automation and web agency for dental and medical clinics: chatbots, booking automation and landing pages that make a clinic easier to reach.

The system should feel trustworthy, sharp, modern. It must never feel like a cheap freelancer, a crypto startup, or a generic tech template. The audience is clinic owners and practice managers in Bangladesh who judge an agency by whether it looks reliable enough to touch their patients. Every surface is clean, precise and calm: one confident blue, near-black ink, generous white space and sharp, small radii. Tagline: Build. Deploy. Grow.

Principles:

1. Clarity before cleverness. Every screen answers one question for a busy clinic owner.
2. Trust is in the details. Aligned numbers, consistent spacing, no decorative noise.
3. Blue means action. The primary color is reserved for things you can click or the current selection.
4. Bilingual by default. Bangla and English sit side by side at equal quality.
5. Show the result. Lead with bookings, replies and time saved, not features.

## Layout

- Spacing scale (4px base): 0 = 0, 0.5 = 2px, 1 = 4px, 2 = 8px, 3 = 12px, 4 = 16px, 5 = 20px, 6 = 24px, 8 = 32px, 10 = 40px, 12 = 48px, 16 = 64px, 20 = 80px.
- Breakpoints: sm 480px, md 768px, lg 1024px, xl 1280px.
- Content max width 1200px on a 12-column grid with 24px gutters. Page gutters 16px on phones, 24px on tablets, 32px on desktop.
- Lay out sibling groups with flex or grid and gap; no per-element margins.
- Radius: none 0, xs 2px, sm 4px, md 6px, lg 10px, xl 14px, full 9999px. Buttons and inputs use md, cards lg, tags sm, avatars and pills full.

## Elevation

Elevation is quiet. Most separation comes from borders and surface color.

| Level | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| e0 | none | none | Flat content |
| e1 | 0 1px 2px rgba(11, 13, 17, 0.06) | 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.40) | Cards |
| e2 | 0 4px 12px rgba(11, 13, 17, 0.08) | 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45) | Dropdowns, chat widget |
| e3 | 0 12px 32px rgba(11, 13, 17, 0.14) | 0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55) | Modals |

Motion: durations fast 120ms, base 200ms, slow 320ms; easing standard `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`, enter `cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0, 1)`, exit `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)`. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing movement and keeping opacity changes only.

## Imagery

No reference images were supplied yet, so no locked image styles exist. Until they do: use real photos of real clinics and staff with permission, natural light, no stock handshakes, no robots, no glowing brains. Product imagery is real UI screenshots on a plain `surface` background.

## Responsive Behavior

- Below 768px, multi-column layouts stack to one column; sidebars become a drawer opened from a menu button.
- Tables scroll horizontally inside their own container; the page never scrolls sideways.
- Touch targets are at least 40px high. The chat widget becomes full-screen below 480px.
- Type scale steps down one level for display and h1 below 768px (display 36/44, h1 28/36).

## Social & Video

Rules for Reels, TikTok, Facebook ads, carousels and video end cards. They use the tokens above; the only addition is the `whatsapp` CTA color.

### Canvas and formats

| Format | Size | Use | Theme |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9:16 | 1080x1920 | Reels, TikTok, Stories (primary) | dark |
| 1:1 | 1080x1080 | Feed posts, square ads | dark or light |
| 16:9 | 1920x1080 | YouTube, link previews | dark or light |

- Video uses the **dark theme**: `bg` #0B0D11 for empty canvas and end cards, `surface` #12151A for panels.
- Carousels and static posts may use the light theme.
- Footage is graded toward the dark theme (slightly cool, deep shadows). Never tint it with a gradient.

### Safe zones (9:16)

- Nothing important goes in the **bottom 15%** (caption, likes, CTA bar) or the **right 12%** (action buttons).
- Headlines sit in the **top 12–24%**. Captions are centred at **64–72%** of the height.
- In 9:16, keep the hook text inside the centre 1080x1080 so it survives the profile-grid crop.

### Captions

- Bangla first: 'Hind Siliguri' 700, **56–64px**, `text` #F1F3F6, soft shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.6)`, no box.
- Animate **per word, never per character**: splitting Bangla text into characters breaks conjuncts (যুক্তাক্ষর).
- 2–4 words per chunk. The active word turns `primary-hover` #8AA9FF (8.5:1 on `bg`) with a 1.0→1.08 scale pop. Yellow or other off-brand highlights are not used.
- English captions: 'Instrument Sans' 600 at the same size.

### Headlines

- Bangla: 'Hind Siliguri' 700, **64–80px**, line-height 1.3. Lines longer than about 18 characters step down to **40–48px**.
- English: 'Sora' 700, **72–96px**, -0.02em tracking, `text-wrap: balance`.
- On footage, a headline sits on a solid `surface` panel at 85–92% opacity (`rounded.lg`, padding 16px 32px), never on a gradient.

### Color in motion

- **Blue means action, here too:** `primary` appears only on the CTA, the active item of a list, and the caption highlight.
- **No gradients:** use solid panels and opacity.
- Semantic colors show **results only**: `success` #3CC48F for booked or confirmed, `danger` #F26B5E for missed calls or lost patients, `warning` #F0A23B for pending.

### Calls to action

| CTA | Fill | Text | Shape | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary pill | `primary` #5683FF | `on-primary` #0B0D11 (5.6:1) | `rounded.full`, min height 44px | "ডেমো নিন", "Book a call" |
| WhatsApp | `whatsapp` #25D366 | #0B0D11 (9.8:1) | `rounded.full`, min height 44px | Only the WhatsApp / inbox button |
| Comment keyword | `surface` + 1px `border` | `text` | comment-box UI | "কমেন্ট করুন DEMO" |

`whatsapp` (#25D366) is a semantic color reserved for that one button. It is never used decoratively.

### Motion

- UI animations keep the standard durations (120/200/320 ms) and easings.
- Video beats use quick **0.15–0.3 s ease-out pops** and **hard cuts**. In showcase edits there are no soft crossfades and at most one new effect per second.
- Every animation must be seek-safe (frame-exact when scrubbed). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` on the web.

### Logo and end card

Files in `brand/futurestack/logo/` (transparent PNG, trimmed with 24px padding):

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo-light.png` | Full logo on white / light surfaces (the original artwork) |
| `logo-dark.png` | Full logo on `bg`, dark footage and dark-mode UI: the gray "S", "STACK", the servers and the navy text become light silver; the blues are unchanged |
| `mark-fs-light.png` / `mark-fs-dark.png` | "FS" monogram only: avatars, watermarks, small corners |
| `logo-original.webp` | The untouched source file |

- Use the light or dark variant to match the background. Never stretch the logo, recolor it beyond these variants, or add effects such as shadows or glows.
- Minimum clear space is 24px around the logo, and minimum width is 240px for the full logo; below that, use the FS mark.
- The logo's own gradients and bevels are allowed: they belong to the mark. The "no gradients" rule applies to UI and layouts.
- End card: `bg` #0B0D11, `logo-dark.png` centred, the tagline **"Build. Deploy. Grow."** in 'Sora' 600, then the CTA pill and contact (WhatsApp number) inside the safe zone.

### Audio

- Voice integrated loudness **-14 LUFS**, true peak ≤ -1 dBTP.
- Music sits about 18 dB under speech (sidechain ducked) and rises slightly in the gaps.
- At most one sound effect ("pop") per on-screen element; no constant whooshes.

### Show the result

Every video shows at least one concrete number on screen, such as bookings, replies, response time or hours saved. Use tabular figures, and Bangla digits in Bangla copy (e.g. "৩২টা নতুন বুকিং").

## Known Gaps

- Imagery styles are placeholders until example images are provided. For video, real UI screenshots on `surface` and real clinic footage are the default (see Social & Video).
- The logo exists as transparent raster PNGs (light and dark variants, plus the FS mark; see Social & Video). A vector (SVG) master is still recommended for print, signage and very large sizes.
- Icon set not yet chosen. Recommended: Lucide (1.5px stroke outline) at 20px and 24px.
- Bangla type scale uses the English sizes on the web; long Bangla headings may need one step smaller. Video sizes are defined in Social & Video.
- No data-visualization palette beyond primary and data-2; add a third series color before building complex charts.
