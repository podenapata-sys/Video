# Effects report: "20 effects in 20 s" reference → Future Stack videos

Source: shot-by-shot breakdown of the reference Reel (videos/style-ref/ref2). Existing implementations live in `videos/futurestack-promo/scripts/build.py`.

## 1. Why the reference looks professional (it isn't only the effects)
1. **One fixed system.** The same panel, the same caption boxes and 2–3 fonts appear in every frame. Effects change; the frame never does. → Our DESIGN.md panel and caption rules do this. Never break it.
2. **Two worlds, hard switches.** The reference cuts between a dark study and cream printed paper. Our version is the dark #0B0D11 brand look and the cream newspaper #F2EDE1. Cutting between the two is what reads as "expensive".
3. **An effect lands on the word.** Every effect starts on a cut and on a spoken word. We already do this with Whisper timings.
4. **Short beats.** About 1 s per effect, with the face clear the rest of the time. Full-screen takeovers last no more than 1 s.
5. **One sound effect per effect.** It's half of the "premium" feel.
6. **The call to action is a moment, not an end card.** The comment drop sits in the middle of the video, at 35–45 % of the runtime, while viewers are still watching.

## 3. Top 5 to add next, ranked by ROI
1. **Banknote**: the clinic owner's ROI in one frame, e.g. "৳৪৫,০০০ / মাস হারাচ্ছেন" (losing ৳45,000 a month). It goes straight at the buyer's motive.
2. **Day dial**: sells the 24/7 chatbot visually in under 1 s.
3. **Layer explode**: shows the whole automation stack (Botpress → Make.com → Google Calendar → WhatsApp) as 3D glass layers. This becomes our "how it works" signature for proposals and ads.
4. **Code body**: proves "we build tech" for agency credibility. A scan line rebuilds the presenter in code characters.
5. **Sketch words**: one hand-drawn keyword per video ("বুকিং", meaning bookings). A cheap way to add style.

Cheap transitions to add to every video: blade slice and rack focus.

## 4. Rules to add to DESIGN.md (Social & Video)
- The face stays clear about 85 % of the time. Full-screen takeovers last no more than 1 s, at most 4 per video.
- At most one effect per 1–1.5 s. Every effect starts on a spoken word and gets its own sound effect.
- Two looks only: brand dark and paper cream (#F2EDE1, ink #1B1712). Cut between them; never cross-fade.
- Comment call to action at 35–45 % of the runtime, plus a short repeat at the end.
- Captions: black or white boxes, max 6–7 words, key word in #8AA9FF.

## 5. How this maps to revenue (mentor verdict)
- **YES**, but only the effects that sell (banknote = ROI, day dial = 24/7, layer explode = system). Each one explains an offer faster than words do, so it raises lead conversion.
- **NO** to collecting effects for their own sake (clone row, orbit ring for decoration, dolly zoom). They cost build time and don't add DMs.
- **Turn it into a product:** package the effect library as a template so each new Reel takes about 1 hour to build instead of a day. Then sell "Reel editing in this style" to clinics as an upsell (৳5–8k per video).
