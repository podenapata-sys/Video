# Content Rewards split-screen video: shot list

Rules from the campaign: **100% human-made** (your real face, your real screen), **say the CTA out loud**, and keep it on screen. I add the on-screen part automatically.

Film everything **vertical, 1080p, 30fps**. Upload the files to me with these exact names.

## Face-cam (top half), with sound
Put the phone on a stand at eye level with a warm lamp behind the phone, and keep the room quiet. Wear a plain dark t-shirt.

| File | What to do | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `hook.mp4` | Stack all three hook types. **Say:** "Today we're gonna try to make a doctor's salary in 67 minutes." **Do:** rapid keyboard clicking, or a big reaction to the camera. | 5–6s |
| `face.mp4` | Give the camera a deadpan stare for about 18s. Glance at the screen a few times and nod once. In the **last 4s, say:** "Comment LINK and I'll send it to you." | 22s |

## Screen recordings (bottom half)
Record on your PC with the **browser at 150% zoom** so it's readable on a phone. Move the cursor fast (another hook the brief asks for) but let each page settle for a second.

| File | Show this for real |
|---|---|
| `s1.mp4` | Type contentrewards.com in the address bar and press enter |
| `s2.mp4` | Click "Start Earning Today" and the sign-up screen |
| `s3.mp4` | Sidebar → "Discover Campaigns" |
| `s4.mp4` | Scroll the campaigns and hover over the pay rate |
| `s5.mp4` | Open one campaign and click "Join Campaign" |
| `s6.mp4` | The submit-your-video screen |
| `results.png` | **Real** screenshot: the campaign's rate ("$1 per 1K views") now. Later, your actual payout. |

Each screen clip only needs 3–5 seconds.

## Never
- AI-generated faces, voices or video. The campaign rejects them.
- An earnings number you didn't earn. Use the rate screen until you have a real payout.
