# "Watch me cook" Reel: shot list

Film everything **vertical, 1080p, 30fps**. Put the files in `assets/footage/` with the exact names below (or just upload them to me), and `build.py` swaps them in.

## Face-cam (top half)
Phone on a stand at eye level, about 60cm away. A warm lamp **behind the phone** lights your face, and the room behind you stays dark. Wear a plain dark t-shirt.

| File | What to do | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `hook.mp4` | One "getting ready" action: open an energy drink and take a long sip, or bite a snack. Stare at the camera while you do it. | 6–8s |
| `face.mp4` | A **silent, deadpan stare** into the lens. Blink slowly and glance slightly left once or twice. Don't smile, don't talk. | 20s+ |

## Screen recordings (bottom half)
Record your phone screen, or your PC at **125–150% browser zoom** so it's readable on a phone. Do the action slowly, and go one step per file.

| File | Show this for real |
|---|---|
| `s1.mp4` | Google Maps: search "dental clinic Dhaka" and scroll the results |
| `s2.mp4` | Open a clinic's website or Facebook page and scroll. No chatbot. |
| `s3.mp4` | Botpress: your demo bot answering "Can I book a cleaning tomorrow?" |
| `s4.mp4` | WhatsApp: send the demo link plus a short message to the clinic |
| `s5.mp4` | Their reply / Google Calendar with the call booked |
| `payout.png` | **A real payment screenshot** (bKash, bank, Payoneer, invoice marked paid). Blur the client's name. |

Each screen clip only needs 3–6 seconds. Captions are in `steps.json`; send me changes in plain text.

## Rules
- **The payout must be real.** No edited numbers. If you don't have a payment yet, end on the booked call ("step 5") and post this video after you close your first client.
- No music in your recordings. Send one MP3 for the background (the references use a slow, moody beat). It goes in as `music.mp3`.
