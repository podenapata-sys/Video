Save as `01.png` … `13.png` and upload all 13 here.

## How to keep the characters consistent
1. Generate the **character sheet** (Step 0) first.
2. For every shot, **attach the character sheet** and paste: *shot prompt* + *style lock*.
3. If a face or outfit drifts, regenerate. The same faces in every shot is what makes it look pro.
