# Clinic ads: read these aloud (phone voice memo is fine)

Record each ad as ONE take, in a quiet room, phone 20 cm from your mouth.
Leave a half-second pause between lines. Save as `voice.m4a` per ad.

## Ad A: problem / agitate (~20s)
1. Your clinic is losing patients while you sleep.
2. Every call you miss after 9 PM goes to the clinic down the road.
3. So we built an AI assistant that answers in seconds, 24/7.
4. It books the appointment straight into your calendar.
5. No new staff. No missed patients.
6. Tap below and book a free 15-minute audit.

## Ad B: proof (~20s). Fill in a REAL client result first.
1. This dental clinic was missing calls every single night.
2. We set up a website, an AI chatbot, and automatic booking.
3. Now it replies to patients in seconds, even at midnight.
4. [REAL result, e.g. "In the first month, they got 23 extra bookings."]
5. Want the same for your clinic? Book a free audit below.

## Ad C: offer (~18s)
1. Clinic owners, I'll audit your patient booking for free.
2. In 15 minutes, I'll show you where patients drop off.
3. Missed calls, slow replies, a website that doesn't book.
4. Then I'll show you the AI system that fixes it.
5. I only take five audits a week. Tap below to claim yours.
