Why is the richest bear in the valley
eating dinner alone?
Barnaby had more gold than anyone.
Three vaults. Forty servants.
And every Sunday, he counted it.
Coin by coin.
His neighbor, Pip, had almost nothing.
A small house. One bowler hat.
But every Sunday, Pip's table was full.
Friends. Laughter.
Bread for everyone.
One winter, Barnaby got sick.
He rang the bell.
Nobody came.
His servants were paid to clean.
Not to care.
So he walked to Pip's house,
through the snow.
And Pip opened the door,
like he'd been waiting for years.
Why did you never come before?
Barnaby had no answer.
Grey, the wolf, watched from the street.
And smiled.
Because he knew something Barnaby didn't.
You can count your gold every Sunday.
But at your funeral,
nobody counts the gold.
They count who showed up.
