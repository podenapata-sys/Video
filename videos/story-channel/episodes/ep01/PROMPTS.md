# Episode 1: The Richest Bear in the Valley — image prompts

Make **18 vertical images (9:16)**. Save them as `01.png` … `18.png` and upload them
all together. Tools: ChatGPT (image generation), Leonardo, Bing Image Creator or
Midjourney.

## Step 1: Character sheet (do this FIRST)
Generate this once. Then attach or reference it with every shot prompt, so the
characters look the same in all 18 images.

> Character reference sheet, three anthropomorphic animal characters standing side by side,
> full body, 1930s rubber-hose cartoon style mixed with modern film-noir lighting:
> (1) **Barnaby** — large old brown bear, round gold spectacles, burgundy velvet smoking jacket
> with black lapels, white cravat, gold pocket watch chain;
> (2) **Pip** — small chubby pink pig, brown bowler hat, worn brown wool coat, mustard waistcoat,
> kind tired eyes;
> (3) **Grey** — tall slim grey wolf, black three-piece suit, crimson tie, white gloves,
> sly half-smile.
> Plain sepia background, consistent proportions, clean line art.
