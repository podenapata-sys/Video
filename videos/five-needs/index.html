FIG. 06 — YOUR PRODUCT
You are capable of selling
if you can write down five things
you think people need the most these days.
Just five.
Take a moment.
Number one: Time.
Everyone is drowning in it.
Tasks, meetings, follow-ups.
If you can give somebody back
even an hour a day,
they will pay you for it.
Number two: Clarity.
Most people don't know what to do next.
They have ten options and zero direction.
If you can show them the one path forward,
you've already sold them.
Number three: Status.
People want to feel important,
recognized ahead of their peers.
Almost every premium product on earth sells this,
whether it admits it or not.
Number four: Certainty.
We live in a world where nothing feels stable.
The person who can promise an outcome and back it up
wins every deal
against someone who can't.
Number five: Connection.
People feel more isolated than ever.
If your product helps them build a community,
or a relationship, or a team,
you're selling something they're starving for.
Now look at your product.
Which of these five does it actually deliver?
Because that's not a feature.
That's the real reason someone will buy from you.
Sell that.
