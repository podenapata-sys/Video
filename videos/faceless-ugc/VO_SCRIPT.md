# Faceless UGC: voiceover script (record this yourself; AI voice is not allowed)

Record on your phone (voice memo) in a quiet room, about 15cm from your mouth.
**Pause about 1 second between sentences.** I use those pauses to sync the subtitles automatically.
Say it like you're telling a friend, not reading an ad. Aim for 20–25 seconds total.

```
This site pays you to post videos.
It's called Content Rewards.
Brands post campaigns, you make the clips,
and you get paid for every thousand views.
This one pays two dollars per thousand views.
Signing up is free. Just join a campaign,
post your clip, and submit it.
Comment LINK and I'll send it to you.
```

There are 8 sentences, and the 8 pauses are what keep the subtitles in sync. If you change the words, tell me and I'll update `script.json`.

Send it as `voice.wav` or `voice.mp3`, plus one background MP3 (optional; it plays quietly under your voice).
