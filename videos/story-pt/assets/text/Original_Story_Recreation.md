# THE 2:14 TEXT — Original Short (style recreation)

Same format as the reference short: vertical 9:16, flat 2D motion-graphics, phone UI inserts, rule cards, spotlight vignettes, bold caps captions with one highlighted keyword, HUD top-left, and a choice card at the end. Story, characters and copy are all new.

**Runtime:** ~77 s · **Format:** 1080x1920, 30 fps, 9:16

---

## Format spec (locked for every scene)

- **Look:** flat vector stick figures, rounded limbs, no faces except a round white head with two dot eyes and thin round glasses. Night palette: background #0F1420 to #1B2233, warm window amber #FFB347, alert red #E63B3B, caption highlight lime #C6FF3D, neutral white #F4F4F4, muted grey #8A93A6.
- **HUD (top-left):** small red dot + "DAY 1", then the clock time in white mono (e.g., "2:14 AM"), then "FLOOR 09" in small grey caps. Changes per scene.
- **Captions:** bottom third, ExtraBold caps, white, with ONE keyword in lime #C6FF3D (red for danger words). Max 2 lines, pops in with a 4-frame scale.
- **Rule cards:** red tag "RULE #N" with the rule in bold white caps beneath. Hold 4 s.
- **Phone inserts:** rounded phone frame, white/grey UI, red alert banner for emergencies.
- **Spotlights:** circular vignette around the character when they're in danger or peeking.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts on caption changes; a 3-frame horizontal wipe on rule cards; a slight zoom-in on reveals.
- **Sound:** low room tone; a phone buzz on each text; a bass hit + swipe on rule cards; footsteps on stairs; a soft knock on the door; a final sustained low drone.

## Scene-by-scene prompts

Each prompt is self-contained: paste the **STYLE LOCK** in front of it.

**01 · Hook.** Night bedroom, side view. A stick figure in a blue coat lies in bed, face up, one arm over the blanket. Phone glows on the nightstand, screen showing "2:14 AM" in white mono. Amber street light through the window. HUD: DAY 1 · 2:14 AM · FLOOR 09. Caption: THE TEXT CAME AT **2:14 A.M.**

**02 · Reveal the sender.** Close-up of the phone on a dark nightstand, a grey message bubble with no contact name, just a long dash for the sender. Message text: "I'm outside. Let me in." Caption: IT WAS FROM **NO NAME.**

**03 · Phone buzzes.** The phone vibrates with three short shake lines, rotates slightly on the nightstand, the screen lights up with a second notification. HUD unchanged. Caption: THEN THE PHONE **BUZZED** AGAIN.

**04 · Emergency alert.** Phone UI insert: full red alert screen with a white warning triangle and "EMERGENCY ALERT" in white caps, sub-line "Stay indoors. Do not answer unknown calls." Button "OK" at the bottom. Caption: STAY INDOORS. **DO NOT ANSWER.**

**05 · Building cutaway.** Side cutaway of a 14-floor apartment block at night, lit windows on every floor. A thin lime outline highlights the 9th floor, with a small figure in its window. HUD: FLOOR 09. Caption: HER APARTMENT IS ON **FLOOR NINE.**

**06 · Stairwell.** Vertical stairwell cross-section, concrete landings, one bare bulb per landing. The stick figure in the blue coat stands at the top of the stairs, one hand on the rail. HUD: FLOOR 09 → 08 (counter ticking). Caption: THE **STAIRS** ARE THE ONLY WAY DOWN.

**07 · RULE #1 card.** Solid near-black background. Red tag "RULE #1" at the top. Below in white ExtraBold caps: DON'T ANSWER UNKNOWN CALLS. A thin red underline sweeps left to right. Caption layer off. HUD hidden.

**08 · Call comes in.** Phone on a stairwell landing, screen shows a green call banner "Mom". A thin lime waveform pulses from the phone. The figure looks down at it, one hand raised. HUD: FLOOR 08. Caption: IT'S **HER MOTHER'S** VOICE.

**09 · RULE #2 card.** Same card layout as scene 07. Tag "RULE #2", text COUNT THE STAIRS. HUD hidden.

**10 · Counting.** The figure walks down the stairwell, each step lights up with a small lime tick. A counter in the corner reads "STEP 40". HUD: FLOOR 06. Caption: COUNT EVERY **STEP.**

**15 · RULE #4 card.** Tag "RULE #4", text NEVER SAY YES TO THE DOOR. HUD hidden.

## Production notes

- **Keep the cadence:** the rule cards sit at roughly 0:07, 0:22, 0:33 and 0:47 in the cut so each one gets a clean breath.
- **Keep the captions:** one caption per 2–4 s. Don't stack two keywords in one caption.
- **Vertical safe zone:** keep HUD 6% in from the edges and captions above the bottom 12%.
- **Original-only:** every character, line, rule and location here is new. Don't reuse the reference's captions, names or rule wording.
